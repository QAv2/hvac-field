Voice test

Five steps, about ten minutes. Run it three times: in Safari, from the Home Screen icon, and from the Home Screen icon in airplane mode. Results from every run collect at the bottom.

1Microphone

Tap, allow the microphone, then talk. The bar should move.

2The phone's own voice

Tap and listen. Then say whether you heard it.

3Offline recognizer

Loads the voice pack (about 47 MB the first time, then from the phone). Then hold the big button, say the words, let go.

4Hands-free callout

Tap once, put the phone down, and just say "field check". Talk normally first if you like — it should only answer to the callout.

5Apple's recognizer (optional)

Tap and say "the walk-in freezer is warm". Shows whether Apple's built-in recognizer works here.
