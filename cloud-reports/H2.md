`cloud/h2-terminal-units`).

## What changed

**New system `terminal`** ("Unit heater, fan coil or baseboard") in `kb/systems.yaml`, with its own `ok_note`
and the shared `ok_signs`. Two context questions live in `kb/terminal.yaml`:
- `tu_heat` — water / glycol / steam / electric
- `tu_type` — unit_heater / cabinet / fcu2 / fcu4 / baseboard / radiant

Fan checks apply only to fan units, pipe and valve checks only to water, glycol and steam, and element checks
only to electric heat.

**`kb/terminal.yaml` — 47 causes, 31 new checks, 5 inputs, 3 measures, 6 synonym groups, 1 new reference.**

| group | causes |
|---|---|
| supply at the unit | no hot water or steam reaching it (points to the Hydronic/Steam system), 2-pipe fan coil not changed over, changeover sensor, 4-pipe chilled-water valve passing |
| valves and piping | service/balancing valve shut, steam supply valve shut or throttled, supply and return reversed, air-bound coil, plugged coil, frozen/split coil, leak at the unit, actuator failed, valve seized, valve stuck open, no control signal at the actuator |
| controls | thermostat in a warm spot, on a cold wall, cross-wired thermostats |
| interlocks and safeties | fan-delay switch stuck open (heat, no fan), fan-delay stuck closed or jumpered (fan blows cold), freeze-stat tripped by cold air, freeze-stat failed |
| fan | motor, capacitor, bearings, fan relay/speed switch/board, loose or dirty blade or wheel, wrong speed |
| air side | filter, matted fins, air path blocked, stratification under high unit heaters |
| steam units | trap failed closed (or its strainer plugged), trap failed open, air vent stuck closed, vacuum breaker missing or failed, condensate piping wrong |
| electric | element open, cycling on the high limit, limit or thermal cutoff open, contactor not closing, contactor welded, line-voltage thermostat open, line-voltage thermostat stuck closed, heater rated above its supply voltage |
| radiant | floor or panel covered, loop supply set too cool |

Measures: supply pipe temperature at the unit (relative to room air), the drop across the coil (optional
design ΔT), and the air temperature rise across the unit (judged against 45 % of water-minus-air temperature,
or 60 °F for steam and 40 °F for electric when no pipe temperature is entered).

## Not finished / known limits

- **Fan-coil cooling mode isn't covered** (condensate overflow, chilled-water valve faults in summer). The
  terminal system's output is "heat", and a cooling complaint would read as "no heat". It needs either a
  second system (fan coil cooling) or a decision to leave it to the A/C side.
- "Baseboard not heating, gurgling" picks only the no-heat chip. `hyd.loop_noise` has no one-word "gurgling"
  phrase. That feature belongs to hydronic.yaml, so I left it alone. "Convector cold" picks no chip either.
  With "gurgling" in the text, the hydronic system (which has "gurgling" as a keyword) also outranks terminal
  for the system pick (4.9 vs 4.1). That is arguably right: gurgling is usually a loop air problem.
- `hyd.water_hammer` is a complaint feature, so it is offered on the terminal complaint screen even for hot
  water and electric units. It is harmless (no cause in play has an opinion on it then), but it is clutter.
- The terminal tile reuses the existing `sun` icon. A new icon would need `docs/js/main.js`, which was off
  limits.
- UFC 3-410-01 was found but not read, so nothing cites it. No DOE page on electric resistance heaters could
  be found. The electric causes rest on practice and P = V²/R (see `research/terminal-units.md`).

## Needs a human decision

1. **Branch name.** The brief says `cloud/h2-terminal-units`. This session's harness required
   `claude/friendly-cerf-faqz6s`, so the work and the PR are on that branch. Rename it if the convention matters.
2. **Overlap with hydronic.yaml.** `hyd.terminal_fan`, `hyd.terminal_blocked` and `hyd.coil_fouled` (and the
   `hyd.terminal_air` check) still model terminal units coarsely inside the hydronic system. They don't compete
   with the new causes (different systems). Options: keep them as the loop-side view, or retire them and point
   to the terminal system in the hydronic `ok_note`.
3. **Keywords moved.** Terminal-unit words now pick the terminal system rather than hydronic. A tech typing
   "baseboard cold, whole building" lands on terminal, where "No hot water reaching the unit" sends them to
   the Hydronic system. Say if you'd rather keep those words on both.
4. **Global synonym.** "blowing cold" → "fan running no heat" is a global synonym (synonyms aren't scoped per
   system). I checked heat pump, AHU, furnace and A/C queries and nothing changed, but it is a shared-file
   effect.
5. **Engine (for H1, no change made).** A system's `output` word is fixed, so one system can't serve both
   heating and cooling complaints on a fan coil. A per-context output (e.g. by a mode question) would let the
   terminal system cover cooling.
